Retrieval review: 2026-09-28. The DPS PDF exceeded the web tool's size limit;
indexed excerpts were available. The 11th-edition Chapter 2C PDF could not be
retrieved, so warning concepts explicitly reference the accessible foundational
2009 chapter. These educational illustrations do not establish the current Texas
installation standard. Independent content review remains necessary before a
public release; machine validation establishes structural integrity only.

SVGs are original educational drawings generated by `scripts/roadready-assets.mjs`.
They use conventional sign shapes, colors and symbols; they are not roadway sign
fabrication specifications. No downloaded third-party illustration is embedded.

The red-arrow item deliberately teaches stopping and checking applicable Texas
restrictions rather than asserting a universal prohibition on every turn.
